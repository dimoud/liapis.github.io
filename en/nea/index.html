News and changes in the law

Changes in laws, deadlines and platforms affecting property, permits and unauthorised works, with their source.

10/7/2026 · Building permits

Transitional-rule applications extended to 30/6/2027

Law 5317/2026 moved the deadline from 30/6/2026 to 30/6/2027 for permits and pre-approvals on properties that met the requirements before 9/12/2020.

Source: ν.5317/2026, ΦΕΚ Α΄ 108/10.7.2026

10/7/2026 · Unauthorised works

Demolitions: priority for orders issued by 30/6/2027

Final inspection reports and demolition orders issued by 30/6/2027 are executed first. If your unauthorised work can be legalised, it is better not to wait.

Source: ν.5317/2026, ΦΕΚ Α΄ 108/10.7.2026

8/6/2026 · Legislation

Spatial and Urban Planning Code in force: where Law 4495/2017 and the Building e-Identity went

Since 8/6/2026 the provisions of Law 4495/2017 on unauthorised works, permits and the Building e-Identity sit in the Spatial and Urban Planning Code (Law 5306/2026). Unauthorised works are in Articles 366 ff. and the Building e-Identity in Articles 341 ff. The substance stays largely the same; what changes are the references in contracts, certificates and files.

Source: ν.5306/2026, ΦΕΚ Α΄ 88/8.6.2026

23/3/2026 · e-Adeies

Building-permit reissue now available on e-Adeies

The platform now accepts the permit-reissue act of article 68, law 5197/2025, for permits annulled or under challenge, provided works had started by 11/12/2024.

Source: ΤΕΕ, ανακοίνωση e-Άδειες 23/3/2026

23/1/2026 · Unauthorised works

Legalising category 1-4 works until 31/3/2028

Article 43 of law 5270/2026 extends applications under law 4495/2017 for categories 1 to 4. The fine is calculated at the start, after the site survey.

Source: ν.5270/2026 άρθρο 43, ΦΕΚ Α΄ 9/23.1.2026

23/1/2026 · Building e-Identity

Building e-Identity: special-use buildings extended to 1/2/2028

The same law sets 1 February 2028 as the deadline for certain categories of special-use buildings and for state and municipal property.

Source: ν.5270/2026 άρθρο 43, ΦΕΚ Α΄ 9/23.1.2026

23/1/2026 · Building code

Permits with building-code bonuses: reissue by 31/12/2026

For permits affected by the Council of State rulings on building-code bonuses, reissue through e-Adeies must happen by 31/12/2026 at the latest.

Source: ν.5270/2026 άρθρο 43, ΦΕΚ Α΄ 9/23.1.2026
