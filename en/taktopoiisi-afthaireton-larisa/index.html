Legalising unauthorised works in Larissa

Preparing a sale or a parental gift, or simply want to know where your property in Larissa stands? We start with a site visit and a calculation of the fine, before you commit to anything.

In short

In Larissa, as across Greece, unauthorised construction and changes of use made up to 28/7/2011 can be legalised against a fine that depends on the category, the area and the official zone value. For categories 1-4, applications are open until 31/3/2028. We start with a site visit and you get the fine in writing before you commit.

Source: ν.5306/2026, ΦΕΚ Α΄ 88/8.6.2026, άρθρο 389

Updated: 13/10/2026

What can be legalised

The Spatial and Urban Planning Code (Law 5306/2026, formerly Law 4495/2017) lets unauthorised construction and changes of use made up to 28/7/2011 be exempted from demolition, against a fine that depends on the category of the breach, the area and the official zone value. For categories 1-4, applications are open until 31/3/2028.

What we come across most

  • Semi-open balconies enclosed to become rooms or kitchens
  • Excess floor area in houses and added storeys
  • Storerooms, canopies and outbuildings on plots and farmland
  • Changes of use, such as a storeroom or basement turned into living space

For each case we work out its category, the fine, and whether legalising it or restoring the original state makes more sense for you.

How we work

  1. Collecting title deeds, permit and drawings, from the building authority if needed.
  2. Site visit, survey and comparison with the permit.
  3. Classification and calculation of the fine, given to you in writing before filing.
  4. Filing on the TEE system, follow-up of the instalments and completion.

Frequently asked questions

What about unauthorised works after 28/7/2011?

They cannot be legalised under the unauthorised-works rules of the Code (formerly Law 4495/2017). In that case we look at whether they can be covered by a permit or whether the original state has to be restored.

I have a declaration under an older law. Is it still valid?

Declarations under Laws 3843/2010, 4014/2011 and 4178/2013 are carried over to the regime of former Law 4495/2017, now in the Spatial and Urban Planning Code, and often need completing. We check yours and tell you what is missing.

Can a building on farmland outside the town plan be legalised?

Often yes, but the terms differ from buildings inside the town plan. We first check the plot’s eligibility and location, then give you an estimate.
