Legalising unauthorised works in Athens and Attica

An enclosed semi-open space, a mezzanine, extra floor area or a change of use. Before anything starts, you learn whether it can be legalised and what it will cost.

In short

Unauthorised works completed by July 2011 and falling into categories 1-4 can be legalised against a fine under the Spatial and Urban Planning Code (Law 5306/2026, formerly Law 4495/2017), with applications open until 31 March 2028. We start with a site visit and you know the fine before you decide.

Source: ν.5306/2026, ΦΕΚ Α΄ 88/8.6.2026, άρθρο 389

Updated: 18/11/2026

Where things stand today

Under the rules of former Law 4495/2017, codified since 8/6/2026 in the Spatial and Urban Planning Code (Law 5306/2026, Articles 366 ff.), unauthorised works in categories 1 to 4 can be legalised against a fine until 31 March 2028. The extension was granted by Law 5270/2026 (Article 43).

Legalisation covers works completed by July 2011. Major violations in category 5 are examined separately, following the rulings of the Council of State.

Legalisation is usually followed by the Building e-Identity, which we prepare as part of the same case.

How we work

  1. On-site inspection and survey of the property.
  2. Comparison with the building permit and the approved plans.
  3. Each violation is classified and the fine is calculated, before you decide.
  4. The file is prepared and the declaration is filed on the Technical Chamber system.
  5. Follow-up until the case is closed.

What we need from you

  • The title deed
  • The building permit and its plans, if you have them
  • Any earlier declarations of unauthorised works
  • The Land Registry number (KAEK) or extract

If something is missing, we request it from the planning office.

Frequently asked questions

How long do I have to legalise unauthorised works?

For categories 1 to 4 the deadline is currently 31 March 2028 (Law 5270/2026, Article 43). Deadlines have changed many times, so we confirm them for every case.

How much will the fine be?

It depends on the category, the area, the zone value and the use. That is why we calculate it at the start, after the inspection, and not at the end.

Can I sell a property with unauthorised works?

A transfer needs the Building e-Identity with its certificate of completeness, in which the engineer records any unauthorised works. If there are any, they are legalised first and the contract is signed afterwards.

Can something built after 2011 be legalised?

Not under the unauthorised-works rules of the Spatial and Urban Planning Code (formerly Law 4495/2017). For newer works a permit may be possible if the construction meets today’s building terms; otherwise the answer is to restore the original state.
