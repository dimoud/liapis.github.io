How we organise a case in Larissa

Most of the work does not need anyone on site. Tracing the permit and drawings, checking title deeds and the Land Registry, the calculations and the filings on the TEE and e-Adeies platforms are all done online. What needs the property itself is the site visit and the measured survey.

The steps

  1. A first conversation by phone or email. You send us the title deed, the Land Registry number (KAEK) and whatever drawings you have.
  2. If you don’t have the permit file, we request it from the competent building authority.
  3. Site visit and survey at the property, on a date we agree together.
  4. Comparison with the approved drawings, a briefing on what we found, and completion of the file.

What we look at especially closely

  • Older houses and two-storey homes with additions that are not on the permit
  • Storerooms and outbuildings on plots and farmland outside the town plan
  • Buildings repaired after the March 2021 earthquakes, to check whether the works are covered by the approval they needed
  • Inherited property, where drawings are often missing and the Land Registry entries need checking

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to come to Athens?

No. Documents are exchanged online and the site visit takes place at the property in Larissa. Anything that needs your signature is signed digitally or by post.

How soon can the site visit happen?

We schedule it around your deadline, for example the date of the contract. If you are in a hurry, tell us in our first conversation.

Is the fee different from Athens?

It depends on the property and the job. Before we start you get a written quote, with the travel cost shown separately.

I am selling a house in Larissa but live in Athens. What do I need from an engineer?

For the contract you need a Building e-Identity with a certificate of completeness and an Energy Performance Certificate. We exchange documents online; in Larissa only the site visit takes place, as long as someone can let us into the property. The certificate of completeness is valid for two months from the site visit, so we schedule it around the contract date.
