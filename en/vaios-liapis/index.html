What he takes on

Most requests concern the Building e-Identity for property transfers and the legalisation of unauthorised works. To date he has completed more than 300 building e-Identities and more than 100 legalisations.

  • Building e-Identity and engineer’s certificate
  • Legalising unauthorised works
  • Building permits and minor-works approvals
  • Energy Performance Certificate (EPC)
  • Topographic surveys
  • Land Registry: checks and corrections
  • Opening shops and cafés
  • Renovation, repairs and insulation
  • Construction supervision
  • Civil engineer for Larissa

How he works

Every case starts with a site visit. Before committing to anything, the owner knows what was found, what is needed, what it costs and how long it will take. For Land Registry corrections he works with a notary and, when the issue is legal, with a lawyer.
