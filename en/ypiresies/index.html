Civil engineering services

One engineer for your whole property case, from the first check to the contract or the finished works. Choose a service to see how we work and what is needed.

Updated: 11/10/2026

  • Legalising Unauthorised Works

    Extra floor area, an enclosed semi-open space, a mezzanine or a change of use: we record what is there and tell you from the start what can be legalised and at what fine, and also what cannot. The file then proceeds under the Spatial and Urban Planning Code (Law 5306/2026) until it is closed.

    More →

  • Building e-Identity

    The Building e-Identity (Articles 341 ff., Law 5306/2026) brings together permits, drawings, legalisations and the actual state of the property. After the site visit we complete it on the TEE platform and issue the certificate of completeness the notary asks for in a sale, parental transfer or gift, the so-called “engineer’s certificate”.

    More →

  • Building Permits

    Building permits and minor-works approvals are now issued only online, through an engineer. Every file starts with a check of plot eligibility and building terms; from there we prepare the studies and follow the file until the permit is issued.

    More →

  • Land Registry Corrections

    We check what the Land Registry says about your property against your deeds. If an area or boundary needs correcting, we prepare the survey that supports it and the application is filed through our partner notary.

    More →

  • Transfer of Declarations to Law 4495/2017

    Unauthorised works declared under earlier laws (3843/2010, 4014/2011, 4178/2013) often have to be moved to the current regime (Law 5306/2026, formerly 4495/2017) before the declaration can be completed or the property sold. Before the transfer we check what was declared back then and what has changed since.

    More →

  • Construction Supervision

    A building permit requires a supervising engineer. We are on site at the critical stages, from excavation to checking the reinforcement before the concrete is poured.

    More →

  • Renovation, Repairs & Insulation

    From a new kitchen or bathroom to external insulation and crack repair. Where a minor-works approval is needed we obtain it, and we supervise the crews until handover.

    More →

  • Energy Performance Certificates

    Energy inspection and Energy Performance Certificate for a sale, a lease or an energy-upgrade programme, for homes and business premises. If the rating comes out low, we show you which improvements raise it at a reasonable cost.

    More →

  • Operating Licences

    For shops, cafés, offices and warehouses we prepare the technical part of the licence: floor plans, engineer’s certificates and, where needed, a fire-safety study. First we check whether the space allows the use you want, before you sign a lease.

    More →

  • Topographic Surveys

    Topographic plans tied to the Greek reference system EGSA ’87, for permits, the Land Registry or boundary changes, measured on site and drawn to the specifications of the authority that asks for them.

    More →

  • Civil engineer for Larissa

    Building e-Identity and legalisation of unauthorised works for properties in Larissa, with a site visit at the property and everything else done remotely.

    More →
