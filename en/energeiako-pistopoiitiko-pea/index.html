Energy Performance Certificate (EPC) in Athens

For a sale or a rental, for a new building and for energy-upgrade grants. Site visit, calculation and registration, with the protocol number the notary and the rental declaration ask for.

In short

An EPC is needed for a sale, a lease to a new tenant, a new building and after a major renovation, including flats under 50 m² (article 12, Law 4122/2013). Its number goes on the contract and the lease declaration.

Source: ν.4122/2013, ΦΕΚ Α΄ 42/19.2.2013, άρθρο 12

Updated: 13/10/2026

When you need an EPC

Under Law 4122/2013 (Article 12), an EPC is required when selling and when renting to a new tenant, as well as after a new building is completed or a major renovation. It also applies to flats under 50 m².

The EPC protocol number goes into the sale contract and the rental declaration. When the property is advertised for sale or rent, the energy class must appear in the listing.

It is not required for storage rooms, parking spaces, industrial buildings and standalone buildings under 50 m². A property being sold also needs the Building e-Identity, which we can prepare at the same time.

How we work

  1. Site visit: dimensions, windows, insulation, heating, cooling and hot water.
  2. A sketch of the spaces, if no drawings exist.
  3. Calculation with the TEE-KENAK software and classification into an energy class.
  4. Registration on buildingcert.gr and delivery of the EPC with its protocol and security numbers.
  5. If you want a better class, we tell you which improvements are worth their cost.

What we need from you

  • The owner’s details and the property address
  • The building permit and drawings, if available
  • Access to every room, the boiler and the air-conditioning units
  • In a block with central heating, the boiler details from the building manager

If there are no drawings, we draw a sketch during the visit.

Frequently asked questions

How long is an EPC valid?

The expiry date is printed on the certificate itself, in the “Valid until” field. If key features change, such as windows or the heating system, it is worth issuing a new one.

Do I need an EPC for a studio under 50 m²?

Yes. Since 1/1/2016 an EPC is mandatory for building units under 50 m² as well (Article 12 §7, Law 4122/2013). Only standalone buildings under 50 m² are exempt.

Is a listed building exempt?

Not from the certificate. Listed buildings are exempt from the minimum energy requirements, but a sale or rental still needs an EPC.

What are the first and second EPC in the “Exoikonomo” programme?

The first EPC records the state before the works and the second confirms the improvement afterwards. Deadlines and subsidy rates change with every round, so we check them in the current programme guide.

What happens if I sell or rent without an EPC?

The law provides for a fine of €1,000 to €10,000 on whoever was required to obtain the EPC (Article 20, Law 4122/2013). The EPC number must also appear in the contract and the rental declaration.
